Your meeting audio stays home

The meeting is recorded on your own computer, and the audio never leaves it. No bot joins the meeting, and nothing is sent to a recording service.

Local AI writes the transcript

Speech becomes text on your PC, with Whisper models on your processor or NVIDIA graphics card. No audio goes to a transcription service.

Summaries from the AI you already pay for

Use an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI or Google, a model on your PC, or the AI command-line tool you are already signed into. Only text goes to it, such as the transcript and your instructions, never the audio.

Your AI app transcribes, the audio stays

Ask Claude, Codex or another AI app on your PC to transcribe an audio or video file. Upshot does it on your computer through its MCP server and sends your AI app only the text. Upshot never uploads the file.

Many languages, Hebrew included

Upshot recognizes the meeting's language by itself. Whisper covers about a hundred languages and is most accurate in the widely spoken ones, and a meeting in Hebrew gets a model trained for Hebrew.

Code on GitHub, free to install

The code is on GitHub. Install it for free for personal use, or in an organization of fewer than 20 people, under the Upshot license. Larger organizations, and anyone offering it in a competing product or service, need a commercial license.

Setup

Connect Google Calendar once. Every meeting arrives ready.

First-run setup offers to connect your Google Calendar. It is optional and read-only: Upshot can’t create, change or delete anything in it.

  1. Connect in setup

    Sign in with your Google Account and allow read-only access to your events

  2. Upshot knows your day

    It knows when each meeting is scheduled, so it is ready to record

  3. Notes that know the meeting

    Each recording of a calendar meeting takes its title, time and the people invited

Named for you

Every recording of a calendar meeting carries its title and time, so your timeline reads like your week.

Who was invited

The people invited appear with the meeting in your timeline.

Summaries that know the agenda

The invitation details (title, times, organizer, the names of the people invited, whether they are optional and whether they declined, the description, the location, and the names and links of attached files) go with the meeting’s text to the AI you chose, so the summary knows what the meeting was for. Email addresses are never sent. You can turn this off, and with a model on your PC nothing is sent.

Calendar data goes from Google straight to your PC, never to us. Of your calendar data, only invitation details leave your PC: those above to the AI you chose and, when you ask it to, your meetings’ details and your calendar’s events to an AI app you connected yourself. You can disconnect at any time. Privacy Policy

How it works

Join the meeting. Upshot takes it from there.

  1. It knows the meeting

    From your Google Calendar, or by noticing that a meeting started, in any app

  2. It records

    Quietly, on your computer. Nobody in the meeting sees a thing

  3. You get the notes

    After the meeting, a summary in your format is in your timeline

As automatic as you like

Let it record every meeting it notices, or just have it show you the meetings it spotted. You can always press Record yourself.

Clear on what you promised

Your words are kept apart from everyone else’s, so it’s always clear what you said you’d do.

Works on an ordinary PC

No special hardware. A graphics card makes it faster; without one, your notes just take longer to arrive.

Automatic recording

It notices the meeting, so you don’t have to remember.

It watches for the signs New

When a meeting app takes the microphone, Upshot checks which app it is, the window title, whether the camera is on, and whether both sides are talking. Enough signs together mean a meeting.

It shows you before it acts

Out of the box it only lists the meetings it spotted, so you can see it gets them right. Switch on auto-record once you trust it, and stop pressing Record.

It would rather miss than intrude

Missing a meeting costs you one click; recording something that wasn’t one costs your trust. Every recording says what made it start, so you can always answer “why did it record that?”

Questions

FAQ

Does my meeting leave my PC?
The recording never does: it’s kept and turned into text on your computer. After that it is your choice — the text goes to the AI you picked, or to a model on your own PC, in which case nothing leaves at all. See how far your meeting travels.
Will the other people see anything?
No. Upshot records from your own computer; nothing joins the meeting. Recording laws differ by country, so tell people you’re recording.
How does it know a meeting started?
It notices when a meeting app starts using your microphone and looks for signs of a real conversation. At first it only shows you the meetings it spotted, so you can see it gets them right. Once you trust it, switch on auto-record. Auto-record is new, and you can always press Record yourself.
Does Upshot use my Google Calendar?
Only if you connect it. Upshot reads your calendar events, read-only and on your computer, to know when your meetings are scheduled and to name them. Calendar data is never sent to us. The invitation goes with the meeting's text to the AI you chose, so the summary knows what the meeting was for; you can turn that off. An AI app you connect can also read your meetings’ calendar details and your calendar’s events, when you ask it to. The Privacy Policy has the details.
Can my own AI app read my meetings?
Yes, if you connect it: Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity, Cursor, Open WebUI and other apps that speak MCP. Ask it “what did we decide on Tuesday?” and it reads the transcript and summary from Upshot on your computer. What it reads goes to that app’s AI, as anything in a chat does. You can turn it off, and Upshot shows what it asked for. The Privacy Policy has the details.
Can the summary go straight into an email?
Yes. Tell Upshot to write it as an email, with your sections, tone and colors. Press Copy, paste into your email, and the formatting comes with it.
Do I need a powerful PC?
No. An NVIDIA graphics card makes it faster. Without one, Upshot waits until the meeting ends so your meeting isn’t slowed down, and the notes take longer to arrive.
Which languages, and which platforms?
Meetings in about a hundred languages, with a model trained for Hebrew. The app itself is in English and Hebrew. Upshot runs on Windows only.
