How it works

Join the call. Upshot takes it from there.

  1. It notices the meeting

    Join as usual, in any app. Upshot spots that a meeting has started, on its own

  2. It records

    Quietly, on your computer. Nobody in the call sees a thing

  3. You get the notes

    After the call, a summary in your format is in your timeline

As automatic as you like

Let it record every meeting it notices, or just have it show you the meetings it spotted. You can always press Record yourself.

Clear on what you promised

Your words are kept apart from everyone else’s, so it’s always clear what you said you’d do.

Works on an ordinary PC

No special hardware. A graphics card makes it faster; without one, your notes just take longer to arrive.

Questions

FAQ

Does my meeting leave my PC?
The recording never does: it’s kept and transcribed on your computer. To write the summary, the text of the conversation goes to the AI model you pick. Pick a local model (Ollama) and nothing leaves at all.
Will the other people see anything?
No. Upshot records from your own computer; nothing joins the call. Recording laws differ by country, so tell people you’re recording.
How does it know a meeting started?
It notices when a call app starts using your microphone and looks for signs of a real conversation. At first it only shows you the meetings it spotted, so you can see it gets them right. Once you trust it, switch on auto-record. Auto-record is new, and you can always press Record yourself.
Does Upshot use my Google Calendar?
Soon Only if you connect it. Upshot will read your calendar events, read-only and on your computer, to know when your meetings are scheduled and to name them. Calendar data is never sent to us or to AI providers. The Privacy Policy has the details.
Can the summary go straight into an email?
Yes. Tell Upshot to write it as an email, with your sections, tone and colours. Press Copy, paste into your email, and the formatting comes with it.
Do I need a powerful PC?
No. An NVIDIA graphics card makes it faster. Without one, Upshot waits until the call ends so your meeting isn’t slowed down, and the notes take longer to arrive.
Which languages, and which platforms?
Hebrew and English meetings, with the app itself in both languages. Upshot runs on Windows only.
